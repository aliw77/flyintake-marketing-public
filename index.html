Why Flyintake

Customers decide whether to trust you at the front counter.

Clipboards, blurry photos on someone's personal phone, and "I'll call you when we know more" don't match the shop you've built. Flyintake gives every vehicle the same organised check-in, every time.

  • Settle "that scratch was already there." Time-stamped photos of all four corners are stored with each vehicle.
  • Stop retyping VINs. Scan the barcode and Flyintake fills in the year, make and model.
  • Cut down on "is my car ready?" calls. Text updates go out from the intake screen, and each message is logged.
  • Find the paperwork later. Bills, receipts and attachments are filed in one folder per vehicle.
Everything at check-in

One screen for the whole intake

Every tool your service advisors and techs need to check a car in, document it and keep the customer updated.

VIN scan and decode

Point the camera at the door-jamb barcode. The make, model and year come back automatically from the NHTSA VIN database.

Four-corner condition photos

A guided, full-screen camera captures the front-left, front-right, rear-left and rear-right of the car. Retakes keep the earlier photo.

Voice-to-work-order

Record the customer's concern or the tech's diagnosis. AI writes the transcript and an editable work-order draft for you to review.

Bills, receipts and attachments

Attach parts invoices, estimates, receipts and documents to the vehicle as the job moves along.

Text updates to customers

Send repair updates by SMS from your shop's own number. Every message is saved in the job's history, and customers can reply STOP to opt out.

Google review requests

When a job is marked Completed, send a review request through a branded short link. You can turn on automatic sending, and each repair gets at most one request.

Customer check-in screen

Hand the tablet to the customer. A full-screen view with large keys lets them enter their name, phone, email and texting consent.

A Google Drive folder per vehicle

Photos, bills and audio sync to the owner's Google Drive in one folder per vehicle, named by make, model, owner and VIN.

Teams and multiple locations

Invite advisors and techs with Google sign-in and give each person an owner, admin or staff role. If you run more than one shop, every location's records stay separate.

How it works

From the parking lot to "your car is ready"

A typical check-in takes a couple of minutes, and every change saves as you type.

Scan the VIN

Open a new intake on any phone, scan the barcode and confirm the decoded vehicle and odometer reading.

Walk the car

Capture all four corners, then let the customer enter their contact details and texting consent on the check-in screen.

Record the concern

Talk through what the customer told you. Flyintake drafts the work order, and your team adds bills and receipts as the job progresses.

Keep them posted

Move the job through In progress, Waiting on parts and Ready for pickup, texting updates along the way. When it's done, ask for a review.

Customers stay informed without phoning the shop.

FAQ

Questions shop owners ask

Do my techs need to install an app?

No. Flyintake runs in the browser on iPhone, Android, iPad and desktop. Open app.flyintake.com and sign in with Google.

Does it replace my shop management system?

Flyintake handles intake, documentation and customer updates. It works alongside your existing estimating, parts and invoicing software.

Where are my photos and documents stored?

Originals are stored in your shop's Flyintake account and copied to the shop owner's Google Drive, with one folder per vehicle under FLYINTAKE_VEHICLES. Flyintake asks only for access to the files it creates in your Drive.

How does VIN scanning work?

Point your phone camera at the VIN barcode on the door jamb or registration. Flyintake reads the barcode and looks up the year, make and model in the public NHTSA database. You can also type the VIN.

How do the AI work orders work?

Record or upload a voice note. Flyintake saves the audio first, then creates a transcript and a draft work order that you can edit. Always review the draft before authorising repairs.

Can I text customers from my own number?

Yes. Each shop gets its own SMS-capable number and keeps its own message history. Customers give texting consent at check-in, and every message includes opt-out instructions.

Can I run more than one location?

Yes. Each shop has its own owner, team, records and texting settings. People who work at more than one location can switch shops from the header.
